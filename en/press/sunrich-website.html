KUGA Inc. relaunches the website of Sun Rich,
a B2B importer of fresh-pressed Moroccan argan oil

KUGA Inc. (Nagoya, Japan; CEO Hitomi Kuga) has redesigned the corporate website of Sun Rich, a Japanese B2B importer and wholesaler of Moroccan argan oil and rose water. The new site, built through KUGA's web-design service Usamimi Web Design, went live on August 25, 2026.

Website: sunrichforyou.com (Japanese)

About Sun Rich

Sun Rich holds an exclusive contract with a partner farm in Morocco and supplies non-roasted argan oil and rose water to cosmetics and beauty companies. Its defining practice is pressing to order: the oil is pressed only after an order is placed and shipped by air while still fresh. According to the company, very few businesses in Japan or elsewhere in Asia handle argan oil at this level of freshness and precision, which is why it is chosen by cosmetics makers and salons that care about raw-material quality.

What we did

The site is built around the brand line "Pressed the moment your order comes in." A black-and-gold visual language conveys both freshness and a premium positioning, and the page walks a first-time visitor through the company's strengths in order: the exclusive farm, the non-roasted process, pressing to order, and air freight.

The site runs on WordPress. The project included HTTPS across the site, redirects from the previous URLs, and a rebuilt inquiry form that leads directly to trade enquiries. Layouts are fully responsive for desktop and mobile.

About Usamimi Web Design

Usamimi Web Design is KUGA Inc.'s website and landing-page production service. From interviews and structure to WordPress development and post-launch support, a small team based in Nagoya works closely with clients across Japan. For a redesign or a new site, reach us through the contact form.

About KUGA Inc.

KUGA Inc. is a small web company based in Nagoya, Japan, that plans, builds and runs its own web services and designs websites for clients. Its services include NETDELINK, a multilingual landing-page generator for immigration lawyers; Dance Map Japan, a nationwide directory of dance studios; and Usamimi Web Design, a website production service. Being small lets the team move fast without cutting corners.
