KUGA Inc. launches Uchikare,
a shared family calendar app for iPhone

KUGA Inc. (Nagoya, Japan; CEO Hitomi Kuga) has released an iPhone app for Uchikare, a shared calendar for families, on the App Store in Japan. It is free and shows no ads. On Android phones and computers, it works directly in the browser.

App Store: Uchikare / Browser version: uchikare.jp / About the app: site.uchikare.jp (all in Japanese)

About Uchikare

Think of the paper calendar hanging next to the fridge. It isn't anyone's in particular; it belongs to the whole family, and whatever is written on it somehow reaches everyone. Uchikare puts that "family wall" on each family member's phone. It is not about showing one another your own schedules; it is about the whole family sharing one calendar. Its English name is Our Wall.

Why we built it

It started when the calendar app Hitomi Kuga's family had been using stopped supporting older phones. So Kuga decided to build one.

Kuga's parents, in their seventies, were the first users. Their verdict: "This is what we always wanted." With their previous app, it was hard to tell where to tap, and events they thought they had added sometimes never got saved. They still use Uchikare every day.

Three promises

  • Usable without instructions: designed around the screen of an older iPhone. The app never shrinks text on its own, buttons are large enough to press with confidence, and plain words tell you what each button will do. Children and older family members alike can use it together.
  • Print it for the fridge: monthly and weekly schedules can be printed as a paper calendar, so family members who don't use smartphones are included too.
  • Safe to use: no ads. Sign-up asks only for an email address, a password and the name you want shown on the calendar.

Key features

  • Only one family member creates the calendar, then invites the others with an invitation link or QR code, so everyone uses the same calendar
  • When a family member adds or changes an event, a notice appears at the top of the screen. You can reply with a one-tap OK, or filter the calendar to show just one person's events
  • Month and week views; daily, weekly and monthly repeating events; Japanese public holidays, rokuyō (the traditional six-day cycle) and Japanese era years. Choose from four themes
  • Sign in with the same email address and password in the iPhone app or a browser, and you see the same calendar
  • Events deleted by mistake can be restored from the list of deleted events within 30 days
