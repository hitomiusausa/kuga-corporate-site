KUGA Inc. relaunches the website of
its web-design service Usamimi Web Design

KUGA Inc. (Nagoya, Japan; CEO Hitomi Kuga) has fully redesigned the website of Usamimi Web Design, its website production service for small businesses. The new site went live on November 12, 2026.

Website: usamimidsgn.com (Japanese)

Why we rebuilt it

Most people who come to Usamimi Web Design have just opened a business or are getting their first website. Many want a site but don't know what to ask for, and the biggest worry is cost.

So the new site has two jobs: to make it clear that you can ask even before anything is decided, and to show the costs before you ask.

What's new

  • Clear pricing: a one-page Light plan (from JPY 98,000 excl. tax) and a Standard plan of about five pages (from JPY 198,000 excl. tax). Photo touch-ups, proofreading, basic SEO setup and mobile-friendly layouts are included in both.
  • Running costs explained: typical yearly costs after launch, such as the server and domain, can be checked up front.
  • Step-by-step process: from the first conversation to launch, so first-time clients know what happens next.
  • An inquiry form on the site: send a question the moment you think of it.
  • Column articles: plain-language explanations, with diagrams, of the confusing parts such as first-year budgets and the difference between a domain and a server.

The design keeps the familiar blue-sky-and-townscape illustration and tidies the palette into blue, yellow and white. The site was rebuilt from WordPress as lightweight static HTML, and existing page URLs were kept.

About Usamimi Web Design

Usamimi Web Design is KUGA Inc.'s website and landing-page production service. From interviews and structure to production and post-launch support, a small team based in Nagoya works closely with clients across Japan. For a new site or a redesign, reach us through the contact form.

About KUGA Inc.

KUGA Inc. is a small web company based in Nagoya, Japan, that plans, builds and runs its own web services and designs websites for clients. Its services include NETDELINK, a multilingual landing-page generator for immigration lawyers; Dance Map Japan, a nationwide directory of dance studios; and Usamimi Web Design, a website production service. Being small lets the team move fast without cutting corners.
