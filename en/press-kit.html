About

Boilerplate
SHORT (ABOUT 50 WORDS)

KUGA Inc. is a small company in Nagoya, Japan, that plans, builds and runs web services and apps, makes websites, and helps small businesses put AI to work. Run by a small team working alongside AI, it turns everyday “wouldn't it be nice” ideas into real products.

LONG (ABOUT 110 WORDS)

KUGA Inc. (くうが株式会社) is based in Nagoya, Aichi, Japan. Founded in January 2024, it plans, builds and runs web services and apps, produces websites, and supports small businesses in adopting AI. Its services include NETDELINK, a multilingual landing-page generator for gyoseishoshi (administrative scrivener) offices; Dance Map Japan, a nationwide portal listing ballroom dance studios and parties; and Usamimi Web Design, a website production service. It also develops and runs its own apps, including Uchikare, a shared calendar for families. It makes active use of AI in development, and its small team brings both speed and care to turning everyday “wouldn't it be nice” ideas into real products.
